## Description

阅藏是一款面向长期佛经阅读的移动阅读器。

你可以检索 CBETA 经藏，打开经文后自动缓存以便短期离线阅读；阅读界面使用简体中文显示，并为长篇经文优化滚动体验。首页以点阵呈现整个经藏的全局阅读进度，帮助你看到已经读过多少，也可以从未读位置继续深入。书签支持记录任意阅读位置，适合在不同经文之间切换阅读。

主要功能：

- 检索 5,005 部 CBETA 文献目录。
- 按需下载经文并本地缓存。
- 简体中文阅读显示。
- 长篇经文的平滑滚动阅读。
- “记到此处”保存精确阅读位置。
- 每部经文显示本地阅读百分比或完成状态。
- 首页点阵显示全局阅读进度。
- 支持从书签、经藏检索、目录和全局进度继续阅读。

文本来源：CBETA XML P5。请在重新发布或分发文本时保留 CBETA 相关署名和来源说明。

## Review Notes

阅藏 is a free Buddhist text reader. It downloads public CBETA XML P5 Buddhist texts on demand and caches opened works locally for offline reading. User reading progress and bookmarks are stored locally on the device in this build. There is no account creation, advertising, tracking, analytics, in-app purchase, user-generated content, or external user-data backend.

The app displays CBETA source attribution in the UI. The app should be distributed as a free, non-commercial reader unless CBETA licensing is reviewed and any additional permission is obtained.

## Privacy Label Draft

Recommended App Store Connect answer for this build: **Data Not Collected**.

Rationale: the app does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send reading progress/bookmarks to a developer server. The app makes network requests only to download public CBETA text files selected by the user.
